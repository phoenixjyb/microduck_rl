Use the explicit `--job lean-replication-packed` declaration in the existing
[lean lesson runner](../../src/mjlab_microduck/stance_lean_lesson.py). Its unique

  tick/update receipts and fixed common checkpoints 64, 128, 192 and 255.
- Same plant, motor, delay, observation/action interfaces, reward, optimizer,
  first-terminal/explicit-reset semantics and strict 0.0873-radian stance gate.
- No optimizer/simulator resume, fixture or pilot-parent promotion, graph,
  perception, video, physical motion or learned-capability flag.

The only physical-runtime implementation axis is the read-only packed finite
predicate. Constructor selection is immutable publicly; the real runtime mode
