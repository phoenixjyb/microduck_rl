exact current clean source, machine/GPU/driver, installed frozen stack, assets
and checker can authorize preparation. Inspect its retained device proof and
full collection/optimizer timing first. A rejected, absent, legacy, differently
sourced or malformed qualification cannot be substituted. The runtime's
`legacy` default and historical lesson/replication paths remain unchanged.

Use the explicit `--job lean-replication-packed` declaration in the existing
[lean lesson runner](../../src/mjlab_microduck/stance_lean_lesson.py). Its unique
protocol, service and artifact prefixes prevent collision with historical runs.

- Learner seeds **577, 587, 593**, in that order, sequentially, never concurrent.
- 64 environments, 24 policy ticks/update, 256 updates, fresh CPU learner/Adam/
  rollout/RNG/simulator state from the exact frozen parent weight export.
- Retain initializer and every `model_0.pt` through `model_255.pt`, ordered
  tick/update receipts and fixed common checkpoints 64, 128, 192 and 255.
- Same plant, motor, delay, observation/action interfaces, reward, optimizer,
  first-terminal/explicit-reset semantics and strict 0.0873-radian stance gate.
- No optimizer/simulator resume, fixture or pilot-parent promotion, graph,
  perception, video, physical motion or learned-capability flag.

The only physical-runtime implementation axis is the read-only packed finite
predicate. Constructor selection is immutable publicly; the real runtime mode
is checked during training and after completion. Launch and completion retain
the exact checker hash/mode; checkpoint identity binds source, launch hash and
plant hash. This is not a different motor or stance curriculum.
